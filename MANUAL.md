# Field CRM — help

The same help is inside the app: tap **⋯** at the top right of Home, then **Help**. It works with no signal. If you change one, change the other.

Plan the week on the PC. Do the calls on the phone. Cloud sync keeps the two in step.

## Getting started

### Joining a team? Start here

1. Do step 1 below, to install the app on your phone.
2. On the phone, open the set-up link you were sent. Open it in Chrome.
3. The app opens at **Cloud sync** with your email filled in. Type the temporary password from the message.
4. Tap **Sign in**.
5. Type the team passphrase. Your admin tells you it separately.
6. Tap **Unlock**. The accounts, belt data and the team's calls arrive by themselves.
7. Tap **Change your sign-in password**.
8. Type a new password twice, then tap **Change it**.
9. Do step 6 below, to add your email address.

Skip steps 3 and 4: in a team, the admin loads the accounts and the belt data for everyone. To use the app on your PC as well, do step 2, then open the same link there.

### 1. Install it on your phone

On an Android phone:

1. Open the app's web address in Chrome on your phone.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.
4. From now on, open Field CRM from its icon on your home screen, not from Chrome.

On an iPhone:

1. Open the app's web address in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open Field CRM from its icon on your home screen, not from Safari. Your data is kept safe there; a Safari tab can lose it after a week unused.

Once it has opened once, it works with no signal.

On an iPhone you can't share a file into Field CRM from another app; use its own import buttons instead. If the microphone button doesn't work, use the microphone on the keyboard.

### 2. Open it on your PC

1. Open the same web address in Chrome or Edge.
2. Bookmark it.

Plan the week on the PC. Do the calls on the phone. Cloud sync keeps the two in step.

### 3. Load your accounts

The app arrives empty. You load your own customers, and they stay on your devices and your encrypted cloud copy.

1. In Dynamics, open the **ANZ Active Food Contacts** view.
2. Export it to Excel (.xlsx).
3. In Field CRM, tap **⋯** at the top right of Home, then **Settings**.
4. Tap **CRM and belt data**.
5. Under **Contact database**, choose the file.
6. Tap **Import CRM export**.

Do this again whenever the CRM changes. Monthly is plenty. With cloud sync on, your phone gets the accounts by itself.

In a team, only the admin does this and step 4. Everyone else gets the accounts and the belt data from the cloud.

### 4. Load the belt data

1. Tap **⋯** on Home, then **Settings**, then **CRM and belt data**.
2. Under **Belt reference data**, choose **Plant_Audit_Template_1.xlsm**.
3. Tap **Import plant audit workbook**.
4. Leave the screen on until it says it has finished. It takes a few seconds.

The newer audit form (FORM v1.2) doesn't import yet. Use Plant_Audit_Template_1.xlsm.

The **plant audit register** (which belts are installed where), if you have it, is under **CRM and belt data** too. The **engineering manuals** have their own section in Settings (see How do I… Get the engineering manuals), and the **zone overrides** file is under **Other imports**.

### 5. Turn on cloud sync

You get the project address, the key and your sign-in from whoever set up the cloud project.

1. Tap **⋯** on Home, then **Settings**, then **Cloud sync**.
2. Paste the **Project address** and the **Publishable key**. A set-up link fills these in for you.
3. Type your email and password.
4. Tap **Sign in**.
5. Type your passphrase. On your first device, type it twice to set it. If you are in a team, type the team passphrase you were given.
6. Tap **Unlock**.

Keep the passphrase in your password manager. Without it nobody, including you, can read the cloud copy.

In a team, **Cloud sync** shows the team's name and its admin. The team shares its call reports, visits, account plans, opportunities, products and saved notes, and the CRM accounts and belt data the admin loads. Your tasks stay yours.

To change the passphrase (on your own, or as a team's admin):

1. In **Cloud sync**, tap **Change the passphrase**.
2. Type the current passphrase, then the new one twice.
3. Tap **Change it**.

Devices already unlocked carry on. New devices, and colleagues, use the new one.

After that it runs by itself: when the app opens, when you come back to it, when signal comes back, and a few seconds after any change. With the phone and the PC both open and online, a change on one shows on the other within a few seconds. **Cloud sync** in Settings says "Live updates: on" while that is working.

### 6. Add your email address

1. Tap **⋯** on Home, then **Settings**, then **Your details**.
2. Type your email address.

Outlook uses it as the organiser on the invites. Without it, the people you invite are left off.

### 7. Do your first call

1. Tap **New** on Home.
2. Tap **Site call**.
3. Type part of the account name, and tap the account.
4. Tick the people you're seeing.
5. Tap **Start the call**.
6. Tap **Belt**, **Project**, **Note**, **Health check** or **Task** to log something.
7. Tap **Done** at the top to save it.
8. When you're ready to send the notes, tap **Create and share** at the top of the call.

## How do I…

### Plan a week

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Pick a **Zone** to see that patch, or click **Search the whole account book**.
3. Click an account, or drag it onto a day.
4. Set the time, how long, what it's for, and who you'll see.
5. Click **Save**.

Drag a visit to another day to move it. The coloured chips filter by focus.

Week and Day run Monday to Sunday. Saturday and Sunday are narrower and shaded. A visit booked on a weekend stays there.

### Put visits in Outlook

1. On **Plan**, click **⋯** at the top right of the calendar.
2. Click **Download to Outlook**.
3. Open the file it gives you, and accept it in Outlook.

Only visits that changed since last time are in the file, and downloading again updates Outlook rather than adding a second copy. The colour on a visit: blue is not in Outlook yet, green is in Outlook, orange has changed since. Download again.

### See my week on the phone

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Tap **Day**, **Week** or **Month**.
3. Use the arrows to move. Tap **Today** to come back.

In Month, each day shows how many visits (navy), tasks (light blue) and quote requests (orange) it has. Tap a day to see it.

### See the team's visits

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Tap **Team** in the row of chips. Your colleagues' visits show, each with their name.
3. Tap a visit. If it has been written up, the report opens to read. If not, the app says whose it is and when.
4. Tap **Team** again to go back to your own.

The Team chip appears only when your team has more than one person. A colleague's visits are theirs to move or change.

### Show only tasks, or find one

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Tap **Visits**, **Tasks** or **Quotes** to show only those. Tap it again to show everything.
3. To find something, tap the search icon at the end of that row and type a word.
4. Tap a result. A task or a quote request opens; a visit takes the calendar to its day.

With Tasks picked, the search looks at tasks only. The filter goes back to All each time you open the app.

### Start a planned visit

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Tap the visit. Or tap **⋯** on it, then **Start the call**.

On the PC, click the visit, then **Start the call** at the bottom. Anything you changed on it is saved first.

The call opens with the account and the people already filled in. Once started, the same button says **Open the call**.

### Log a call that wasn't planned

1. Tap **New** on Home.
2. Tap **Site call**, **Phone call** or **Teams call**.
3. Type part of the account name, and tap the account.
4. Tick the people, and tap **Start the call**.

It goes on the calendar by itself, at the time you started it.

### Book a visit for later

1. Tap **New** on Home.
2. Tap **Book a visit**.
3. Tap the account.
4. Pick the day.

### Close out, move or cancel a visit

1. Tap **Plan** on Home.
2. Tap **⋯** on the visit.
3. Tap **Close out**, **Move**, **Missed** or **Cancel**.

Close out is for a visit with nothing to write up. It still counts as a visit. The same menu has the contacts' numbers, and **Reopen** for a visit that's already closed.

### Log a belt, note, project or health check

1. In the call, tap **Belt**, **Project**, **Note** or **Health check**.
2. Fill it in.
3. Tap **Done** at the top.

A note saves itself to the call log as soon as it has text, and a project as soon as it has a name. Every change saves as you type, and the form says so at the top. Done takes you back. Rub out a new one before you leave and it's taken back out.

A belt or health check saves on Done. Leave one with nothing typed and nothing is saved. Leave it half filled and it's kept as a draft for next time. A health check needs a severity.

The belt form is in sections: Belt data, Sprockets, Flights and sideguards, Photos and comments, and Quote contact. Tap a section's name to open or close it. A closed one shows a line saying what's in it. A new belt opens on Belt data; a belt you've already logged opens with them all closed.

### Talk into a box, or tidy a note

Every free-text box on a call has a microphone beside **Tidy**: the note, a project's next action or notes, a belt's comments, and a health check's fault, recommended action or comments. So does a task's notes, and every text box on an opportunity's map and Blue Sheet, the account plans, saved notes, products, a visit's agenda and your CIP.

1. Tap the microphone.
2. The first time, allow the microphone when the phone asks.
3. Talk. Your words appear in the box as you speak.
4. If you stop to think, it pauses and keeps your words. Tap **Continue** to carry on.
5. Tap **Finish** when you've finished.
6. Wait a few seconds. Claude's version shows above yours.
7. Change anything you want in it.
8. Tap **Use this** to put it in the box, or **Keep mine** to keep what you said as it is.

Or hold the microphone while you talk, and let go when you've finished. While you hold it, it keeps listening through pauses.

Chrome sounds an alert each time the microphone starts listening. Pausing doesn't start it again; only **Continue** does.

Say how you want it laid out as you go: "make that a bullet list", "scratch that", "delete the last sentence", "new paragraph". Claude does it and leaves those words out.

To tidy something you typed, or dictated with the keyboard's microphone:

1. Tap **Tidy** under the box.
2. Wait a few seconds, check Claude's version, then tap **Use this** or **Keep mine**.

It fixes punctuation and voice-typing mistakes and turns several points into bullets. It keeps your facts and numbers and adds nothing. Check it anyway.

The microphone uses the phone's own speech recognition, which needs signal and keeps no recording. If there's no microphone beside Tidy, this phone's browser doesn't offer it; the keyboard's microphone still works.

It needs signal and cloud sync signed in. The text in that one box is sent to Anthropic (the company behind Claude) to be tidied, without the app's encryption. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent unless you tap the button.

If it says the AI key has not been added, it hasn't been set up yet.

### Add a summary, or tidy the whole call

1. In the call, tap **Summary for the report** near the top.
2. Tap **Write summary**.
3. Wait a few seconds. The AI writes a short summary of the visit and the next steps from everything you've logged.
4. Change anything you want, then tap **Use this**.

The summary goes at the top of the full call notes. It isn't put in the health check or belt requirements documents. You can also type or dictate it yourself, and change it any time.

To tidy every box in the call at once, tap **Tidy all notes** in the same place. Each tidied box is listed and ticked. Untick any you want to keep as they are, change any wording, then tap **Use ticked**. **Keep all mine** changes nothing.

Each box tidied counts towards the 200 a day.

### Read a paper spec sheet

1. In the call, tap **Belt**.
2. Tap **Read a spec sheet**.
3. Take a photo of the sheet, or choose one from your photos.
4. Wait about 5 to 10 seconds.
5. Check every tinted box. Change anything that's wrong.
6. Tap **Done**.

Only empty boxes are filled. Anything you'd already typed or picked stays as it is. A tinted box goes back to normal once you change it.

The line under the button says what was filled, what was left alone, and anything it couldn't read. Series, style and materials have to match your belt reference data; anything that doesn't is listed for you to pick.

The photo of the sheet is kept with the belt. It needs signal and cloud sync signed in, and the photo is sent to Anthropic to be read. It counts towards the 200 a day.

### See what you are working on

1. Tap **Strategy** on Home.
2. Tap **Large accounts**, **Blue Sheets** or **Account plans**.
3. Tap one to open it.
4. Tap the back arrow to come back to the list.

The tabs follow the order the work goes in: the large account plan leads, its Blue Sheets are the opportunities that need particular relationships to reach an outcome, and the sites' account plans take their lead from it. Large accounts show their sites, Blue Sheets and site plans, and when they were last reviewed. Blue Sheets are listed by close date, nearest first, with their position and red flags. Account plans show their goal, last changed first.

In a team, **Mine** shows your own and **Team** your colleagues'. The search icon looks through all three lists, everyone's, by name, account or person.

To start a new one from here:

1. On **Large accounts**, **Blue Sheets** or **Account plans**, tap the circle-plus on the tab.
2. Type the account's name, then tap it. For a large account, pick one of the customer's sites; the plan takes in its other sites.
3. Fill it in, then tap **Done**. Back comes to Strategy.

If the account already has an account plan, or the site is already in a large account, the list says so and tapping it opens that one.

### Bring in your CIP and MBOs

1. Tap **Performance** on Home, then the **CIP** tab.
2. Tap **⋯** at the top right.
3. Tap **Import a CIP and MBO file**.
4. Pick the file the CIP and MBO page saved with **Save HTML copy**. It brings everything: your plan, your MBOs, the four pillars and the reference. A file saved with **Save to file** works too, but holds no reference.
5. If the app asks whether to replace what is there, tap **OK**.

Do this on one device. Cloud sync takes it to your others. Your CIP and MBOs are yours alone: they never go to your team, the admin included. They are not in the app itself either, so each person brings in their own.

### Keep your CIP

1. Tap **Performance** on Home, then the **CIP** tab.
2. Pick the period at the top, for example **2026 H2 year-end**.
3. If it hasn't been started, tap **Start**. Starting from the period before carries your goals and development plan forward.
4. Type into the boxes. Everything saves as you go.
5. To fill a box from the list, pick from the list above it. The words drop into the box and can be changed.
6. To see what you wrote before, tap **Compare with** and tap a period. What you wrote then shows in grey under each box.
7. When the review is done, tap **⋯**, then **Lock**. Tap **⋯**, then **Unlock** to change it again.
8. To send it, tap **Create and share**. Tick the box to add the reference at the end, then tap **Create and share**.

Each goal, strength and gap can be locked on its own with **Lock**, and opened again with **Edit**. The bin removes one, after asking.

**Four pillars** shows how your time splits across your role. Type the share now and the target for each pillar. Tap **+** to add a bubble, and tap a bubble to write its plan or move it to another pillar. On the PC you can also drag bubbles. **Reference** shows the job indicants, the LCI principles, the BU goals and the booklist.

### Log your MBOs

1. Tap **Performance** on Home, then the **MBO** tab.
2. Pick the year at the top.
3. On a phone, tap the quarter: **Q1**, **Q2**, **Q3** or **Q4**. The PC shows all four.
4. Tap **+** in an MBO's row to log a completion.
5. Type the customer, site or book. Add the date and the proof reference if you have them.
6. Tap **Done**.
7. When a quarter is submitted, tap **Lock** at the top of its column.
8. To make the claim, tap **Create and share**, pick the quarter, then tap **Create and share**.

Each quarter pays up to a quarter of the annual cap. Anything over carries to the next quarter, and whatever is still over after Q4 is not paid. A Mega MBO marked **Met** pays its share of the cap on top. A locked quarter keeps the values it was locked at, even if the list changes later.

Tap an MBO's name to see the proof it needs. To change the list, tap **⋯**, then **Edit the MBO list**. If the list is locked, tap **⋯** and unlock it first. A new year starts by copying last year's list.

To plan next year's MBOs while this year's run:

1. Tap **⋯** on the MBO screen.
2. Tap **Plan the** next year's **MBOs**. It starts from this year's list, with no values.
3. Tap **⋯**, then **Edit the MBO list**, and add, change or remove MBOs. This year's list is not touched.
4. To make an initiative one of next year's MBOs, tap its name, then **Make it one of the** next year's **MBOs**.

Next year's list says **Planning** until 1 January, then it is that year's list. Add the values once they're agreed.

### Count calls towards your MBOs and initiatives

1. In the call, tap **MBOs and initiatives**, under the summary.
2. Tick each one this call counts towards.
3. Tap **Done**.

A tick goes into the call's quarter (January to March is Q1), with the account and the date. Untick it to take it out. Moving the call to another date moves its ticks. A locked quarter can't take new ticks.

To have one tick itself:

1. Tap **Performance** on Home, then the **MBO** tab.
2. Tap the MBO's or initiative's name.
3. Under **Ticks itself when a call**, choose what the call must be: the account's focus, the call type, a health check, a project, a project moved to Complete, or a belt mentioning a product.
4. Tap **Close**. Calls already made this year are counted too.

Everything you set must be true for the tick. Untick a rule's tick on the call, or delete it on the MBO screen, and the rule leaves that call alone.

Initiatives sit under the MBOs and earn nothing. To add one, tap **Add initiative** at the bottom of the MBO screen. To make one count towards an MBO, open it, pick the MBO under **Counts towards an MBO**, and pick the quarter it starts from. From then on a call ticked on either counts on both, and is paid once.

A task can count too: open the task, pick it under **Counts towards**. It counts in the quarter you tick the task done. A CIP goal with a related MBO shows that MBO's count for the year.

Your MBOs, initiatives and ticks are private. They are kept with your MBO list, never on the call, so your team never sees them.

### Map a large opportunity

1. Open the account: tap **Directory** on Home, then the account.
2. On the **Opportunities** card, tap **New opportunity**.
3. Type its name, then tap **Done**. The map opens with the end user (the account) and Intralox already on it. Or describe it instead: see **Set up an opportunity by describing it**.
4. In the list beside the map, tap the circle-plus next to **Head contractors**, **OEMs and integrators** or **Consultants**.
5. Type the company's name, or pick it from your customer list, then tap **Add**.
6. Tap the table icon next to a party to add its people.
7. Type one person per row. Only the name is needed. Add their title, who they report to, their part (E, U, T or C) and whether they are helping us if you know them.
8. Tap **Done**.
9. Tap the circle to the right of each party's name to say whether it has specified Intralox: once for **Specced**, again for **Unsure**, again for **Not specced**.
10. To link two people, tap one, tap **Link to…**, then tap the other, or the opportunity.
11. Add red flags and strengths in the panel, and pin each to a person or a link.

Each party has its own colour: the end user dark blue, head contractors dark cyan, OEMs and integrators grey, and Intralox red, around the opportunity in orange. People hang under their party as an organisation chart, each under who they report to. A party that has not specified Intralox, or is unsure, has a dashed orange edge, so it stands out.

Tap a party to say where a head contractor stands (Bidding, Appointed or Out of it), who an OEM works for, and which belting an OEM prefers. An OEM can work for a head contractor or for another OEM or integrator, so a conveyor builder under a line integrator under the head contractor draws as a chain. Under **Works with**, an OEM or a consultant ticks the other parties it deals with and how (for a consultant, **Specifies for** or **Advises**); each draws its own line. A consultant is an engineering firm or designer working with the end user and the OEMs. An OEM, contractor or consultant that is one of your customers in the CRM shows its account manager, who covers its people by default as at a site. On the Blue Sheet, anyone with a part in the decision whom nobody of ours covers is listed as a gap, wherever they work. Intralox is ticked to start; tap a supplier to add or take it off, or type another and tap **Add**.

Tap a person to see their relationships on their own: everything else fades. Their role, view, rating, win and evidence are under **More detail**. Tap the opportunity to see everyone again.

At the top of the list, **People shown** hides everyone's people, and **Links on** hides the relationships between people. The eye next to a party hides just its people. The list keeps each party's people folded away: tap the arrow at the left of a party to show them, and again to fold them away. On a large account's map the list names a site without the account's own name, so **Sample Foods - Northside** reads **Northside** there; the map shows the full name.

On the PC, drag a party, or the opportunity, to move it; its people move with it. On the phone the list folds above the map; tap **Parties and people** to open it. Drag the background to move around, and use − and + to zoom.

Everything saves as you go, and the team shares it. To add the objective, value and close date, change the name, or record how it ended, tap **Details**. A map made before this change is sorted into parties the first time it opens.

Every text box on the map and the Blue Sheet has a microphone beside it. Tap it, talk, then tap **Finish**: Claude tidies what you said, and you check it before it goes in.

To delete the opportunity, tap the bin at the right-hand end of the bar above the map, or the bin on its card on Strategy or on the account. It says what will be lost and asks first.

### Set up an opportunity by describing it

1. Open the account, then tap **New opportunity** on the **Opportunities** card.
2. Type a name if you have one.
3. Under **Or describe it**, tap the microphone and say what the project is: who it is for, the head contractor, the OEMs, and anyone you know at each. Tap **Finish**.
4. Claude reads it. Check the list: each company and person found is ticked.
5. Untick anything that is wrong.
6. Tap **Make the map**.

Companies are matched to your customer list where they can be, even if the voice typing misspelt them a little. A company with several sites needs the site said. Anyone new at one of your customers joins its contacts, marked **Not in Dynamics yet**. Someone already on another Blue Sheet brings their part and stance as estimates.

To add more to a map later, tap **Add from a description** at the top of the list beside the map. Parties and people already on it are not added twice.

It needs signal and cloud sync. Only the description and the customer's name go to Claude, never your customer list.

### People met on a Blue Sheet

Anyone you add to a map under one of your customers joins that customer's contacts, marked **Not in Dynamics yet**. The team sees them too. They stay through every CRM import until Dynamics has the same name at that customer, then the mark goes by itself.

They go to CAG with the other changes: see **Owners, moved contacts and changes for Dynamics**.

When you type a name into a party's table and that person is on another Blue Sheet at the same company, their part and whether they helped us are filled in from the latest one. These boxes are dashed: they are estimates. Change one and it is yours; leave it and it stays marked. On the map, tap the person to see the estimate and their other Blue Sheets. Choosing their part or stance there confirms it.

To record how an opportunity ended:

1. Open the opportunity and tap **Details**.
2. Under **Outcome**, tap **Won**, **Lost** or **On hold**.
3. Tap **Done**.

The outcome shows on Strategy, goes with each person's history into the next Blue Sheet, and the coach reads both.

### Fill in the Blue Sheet

1. Open the opportunity from its account.
2. Tap **Blue Sheet** at the top.
3. Fill in the salesperson, the current volume and the total potential volume.
4. Under **Where we stand**, set the adequacy of our position from −5 (panic) to +5 (euphoria), and type it in a word, for example Concern.
5. Tap one chip in each row: our position against the competition, the place in their funnel, and the timing for priorities.
6. Under **Ideal customer criteria**, type each criterion and tap **Add**. Pick how well they match, from −5 to +5. The total adds itself up.
7. Under **Buying influences**, tap a person to open them. Set their part in the decision, and a second part if they have one. Set how much they sway it and how they see it.
8. Set how well our base is covered with them, from −5 to +5, and type the evidence for it.
9. Type their personal win and the business result they want.
10. Under **Gaps**, tap **Add as a red flag** for any gap that matters. Under each red flag, type the information needed and who it is from.
11. Under **Possible actions**, type each action and tap **Add**. Type who does it and when.
12. Tap the star on each action in the best action plan.
13. To put an action in your calendar, tap **Make a task** next to it.
14. Under **Communication notes**, pick the date, type who you spoke to and what about, then tap **Add**.

Who covers each person comes from the map: link one of ours to them with **Our coverage**. The Gaps list shows what is missing: nobody who signs the order, no coach, someone not covered, or a part not known.

On the map, the opportunity shows where we stand, a card shows both parts (E/T), and the stripe down a card shows how well our base is covered with that person. With nothing selected on the map, the panel beside it has the same three rows of chips.

The Blue Sheet works the same on the phone and the PC. Tap **Map** to go back to the map.

To get coaching on it:

1. On the Blue Sheet, tap **Coach**. It needs signal and cloud sync.
2. Read where it stands, the biggest risks, who to see next and the questions to ask.
3. Under **Suggested for the Blue Sheet**, tick the red flags, strengths and actions you agree with.
4. Tap **Add ticked to the Blue Sheet**.

The coaching is kept with the opportunity, so **Coach** shows it again with no signal. After the sheet changes, tap **Write it again**. The coaching is never in the Blue Sheet document.

### Send a Blue Sheet

1. Open the opportunity, then tap **Blue Sheet** at the top.
2. Tap **Create and share**.
3. Tap **PDF** or **Excel**.
4. For a PDF, tick the box if the relationship map should go too.
5. Tap **Create and share**.
6. For a PDF, the print screen opens. Choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
7. On the phone, tap the round **PDF** button. On the PC, click **Save**.

Both follow Miller Heiman's own Blue Sheet, in its order and with its letters, so anyone who knows the form can read it. The PDF is A4 and is named after the account and the opportunity. The Excel copy is laid out in the template's own cells, so it opens in Excel and can be imported back. Excel can't carry the map. The map is left out unless you tick it, because it is your planning view.

### Import a Blue Sheet

1. Open the account: tap **Directory** on Home, then the account.
2. On the **Opportunities** card, tap **Import a Blue Sheet**.
3. Pick the Blue Sheet file, either .xls or .xlsx.
4. The map opens with everyone from the sheet in their lanes. On the PC, drag them where you want them and link them.

It brings in the objective, the position and its ticked boxes, the ideal customer criteria, the buying influences with their roles, influence, mode, rating and evidence, the strengths, the red flags, the possible actions, the best action plan, and the communication notes. Anyone the sheet names as an OEM or consultant goes in the OEM lane. The opportunity goes on the account you are on, whatever account the sheet names.

### Plan a large account

1. Open one of the customer's sites: tap **Directory** on Home, then the site.
2. Tap the **Account plan** card.
3. Tick **Large account: several sites under one plan**.
4. Tap **Done**. The large account opens on its own map. Each site has its own box, with the people from its opportunities under it.
5. If a head office or group account drives the sites, tap the circle-plus next to **Corporate** in the list beside the map, then type its name or pick it from your customer list.
6. If the head office is at one of the sites, tap that site on the map instead, then tick **Head office here**.
7. To add another site, tap the circle-plus next to **Sites** in the list beside the map, pick the customer, then tap **Add**.

3. In a team, pick **Whole team** or **Mine**.
4. Pick the **Accounts**: every account, or one manager, focus level or zone.
5. Pick **Full**, or **Condensed** for a shorter read.
6. Tick **Hide contact names** if people's names should not go.
7. Tap **Create the export**.
8. Send the files to OneDrive or to yourself. On the PC they go to Downloads.
9. On claude.ai, make a Project and add every file to it.
10. Start a chat in the Project and ask a question. The read-me file suggests where to start.

The files are text: any large account plans first, then each account's details and plan, its opportunities with their Blue Sheets, and its calls, oldest first. Opportunities are included whatever the period, because they are live now. Photos and test calls are never included. A big export comes in several parts; add them all.

The files hold customer details. Send them only where the company allows.

### Back up, or restore a backup

1. Tap **⋯** on Home, then **Settings**, then **Backup and restore**.
2. Tap **Back up**.
3. Save the file somewhere that isn't the phone.

To restore, choose the file under **Restore** and tap **Restore from backup**. It adds and updates. It never deletes anything already there.

Cloud sync isn't a backup: a delete reaches every device. The backup file is your undo.

### Set up a new phone

1. Install the app (Getting started, step 1).
2. Turn on cloud sync with the same sign-in and passphrase (step 5).
3. Leave it open for a minute with signal.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything on that phone. Make sure it has synced, or back up, first.

### Something's not right

**A fix isn't there yet.** Get the latest version (above), or close the app fully and open it again.

**Nothing shows on Plan.** Either nothing is planned, or this device hasn't synced yet. **Cloud sync** in Settings says when it last synced.

**Sync failed.** The line under Cloud sync says why, usually no signal. It tries again when signal comes back.

**An account is missing.** It wasn't in the export. Check the view in Dynamics, then import again.

**Field CRM isn't in the share list.** It only appears once the app is installed from Chrome (Getting started, step 1).

**The belt pickers are empty.** Load the belt data (Getting started, step 4).

## Two things to remember

**Never put an export, a backup or the zone overrides file in the GitHub repository.** It's public. Customer data lives on your devices and, encrypted, in your own cloud project.

**The passphrase can't be recovered.** Keep it in your password manager.
