Notes

  • Walked three conveyors with Jo Bloggs, from the infeed (CV-100) to packing (CV-300).
  • CV-100 infeed: carryway wear strips worn at the transfer end, about half their height left. Example Foods maintenance will replace them at the next shutdown. Belt, tracking, sprockets, shafts and return way are fine.
  • CV-200 spiral exit: rods walking out on the left side about every third row, with worn hinges, since the wash-down change about six weeks ago. Drive sprocket teeth are hooked. A new belt and sprockets are recommended (Belts to quote, below).
  • CV-300 packing: belt tracking to the right and rubbing the side frame near the outfeed. Not urgent; check the take-up adjustment at the next service. Hygiene is good.
  • Second packing line: Pat Example is planning a second packing line next year, about 30 m of conveyor. Design starts in March, installation over the Christmas shutdown. Intralox to help with the layout.
  • Follow-ups: quote the CV-200 belt and sprockets by Friday 30 October (Alex Example). Send the spiral and transfer case studies to Pat Example next week (Alex Example).
