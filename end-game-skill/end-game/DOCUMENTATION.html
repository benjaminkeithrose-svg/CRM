Claude skill

End game: documentation

Version 1.0, 29 October 2026.

A Claude skill for Claude Enterprise on the PC. It writes the call report for a site call from the call in Field CRM, the Plaud recording, the phone photos and the handwritten notes, files everything in the visit's folder, and hands the report back to Field CRM. Everything in the examples is made up. It is the sibling of the site-walk-report skill and shares its tools for matching photos and drawing documents; the two do not touch each other.

1. What it does

You log the call in Field CRM as usual and walk the plant with the Plaud recording. Back at the PC you press Save for Claude on the call, then type Do the call report in Claude. Claude:

  • reads the call Field CRM saved: the customer as the CRM account, the contacts, the belts you logged (with their photos) and your notes;
  • fetches the Plaud recording, and collects the visit's photos from your camera roll by the time they were taken;
  • works out which conveyor each photo shows, from when it was taken and what was being said, and captions it;
  • reads the handwritten notes, data sheets and drawings;
  • condenses everything discussed into short bullet-point notes, with the agreed follow-ups, and keeps anything internal apart;
  • completes the belts to quote: what you logged in Field CRM, plus what the walk added (a width said out loud, the sprockets);
  • draws the documents and saves everything in the visit's folder;
  • writes fieldcrm-import.json, which Field CRM's Import call report lays back into the same call;
  • ends with a short list of things for you to check.

What you get, in the visit's folder

Site walk notes (internal) Site walk report (customer copy)
Details Call details: date, call type, account manager, customer, site; "Not in CRM" flag Visit details: date, customer, site, Intralox contact
People Contacts: name, role, email, mobile, CRM status People: name, role
Notes Yes Yes
Internal notes Yes No
Photos Yes Yes
Belts to quote Field CRM's quote request: required fields shown even when empty, quantity first, TSG line The same tables, only what was given

Each as HTML (to email) and PDF (A4). And fieldcrm-import.json for Field CRM: the report, its belts, photos, contacts and follow-ups, and the two documents to keep with the call.

2. How it works

 Field CRM: Save for Claude ─► Field CRM call.json + from Field CRM\ (belts, photos, contacts)
 Plaud recording ──────────► transcript with times ─┐
 camera roll ─► collect_photos.py ─► photos\ ───────┼─► walk_timeline.py ─► timeline.json
 handwritten notes, data sheets ─► read by Claude    │
                                                     ▼
                                             source\report.json
                                     │                              │
                            build_report.py                  make_import.py
                                     │                              │
              Site walk notes + Site walk report          fieldcrm-import.json
                    (HTML + PDF)                                    │
                                                Field CRM: Import call report
                                                (the same call; syncs encrypted)

The visit folder is the meeting point. Field CRM's PC folder already files each call as Customer\Site\YYYY-MM-DD. Save for Claude writes the call there; Claude reads it there and leaves its files beside it; Import call report reads Claude's file from there. Nothing passes between Claude and Field CRM's database: files only. Claude never needs Field CRM's key, and the imported call syncs encrypted like any other change.

Field CRM is the record; the folder holds the documents. The import puts the report into the call as data (the notes, real belt entries, photos, contacts, next steps), so it is kept permanently with every other call and can be searched and reported on later. The documents are kept with the call too, without their photos (those are on the call already).

Times line the walk up. The recording's start time plus each remark's offset is the clock time of every remark; each phone photo carries the clock time it was taken. A photo sits with whatever was said at that moment, under whichever conveyor was last named before then. Photos taken in Field CRM are already on their belt.

Claude decides the words, the scripts decide the look. Claude fills in report.json; build_report.py draws it with Field CRM's own stylesheet and belt fields, so every report looks the same.

A person checks it. Claude lists anything doubtful before you send.

3. Setting it up

What is needed

  • Claude Enterprise with Code execution and file creation turned on, and Skills turned on (an owner turns skills on for the organisation).
  • Claude Desktop with access to your PC folders: the Field CRM PC folder and the OneDrive camera roll folder. This is what lets Claude read the call and the photos and save the documents where they belong. Without it, see section 5, "Without folder access".
  • Field CRM v186 or later, with its PC folder set up (Reports, Save to PC folder, on the PC in Chrome or Edge).
  • OneDrive camera upload on the phone, so the photos reach the camera roll folder on the PC with their own times.
  • The Plaud connector, so Claude can fetch the recording. Without it, export the transcript from the Plaud app.
  • Your CRM export in a PC folder, only for calls not logged in Field CRM.

Installing it

  1. In Claude, click your name, then Settings, then Capabilities.
  2. Turn on Code execution and file creation.
  3. Under Skills, click Upload skill and choose this zip file, as it came (not unzipped).
  4. Check end-game shows in the list, turned on.

An organisation owner can add it once for everyone instead, from the organisation's admin settings (Skills).

The settings file

The first time, Claude asks for three places and offers to remember them in end-game settings.json in your Field CRM PC folder:

{"pc_folder": "C:\\Users\\you\\OneDrive - Intralox\\Field CRM",
 "camera_roll": "C:\\Users\\you\\OneDrive - Intralox\\Pictures\\Camera Roll",
 "crm_export": "C:\\Users\\you\\OneDrive - Intralox\\CRM export\\contacts.xlsx"}

The paths are examples. Change the file whenever one moves.

Checking it works

Ask Claude: Test the end-game skill with its test visit. To test only the tools, run python3 scripts/self_test.py from the skill's folder: it prints ok or FAIL for each check, through to the file for Field CRM.

4. On site: habits that make good notes

These matter more than anything else. The tools can only match what the recording and the photos give them.

  1. Start the Plaud before the first conveyor, and leave it running for the whole walk. One recording per walk.
  2. Say the customer, the site and who you are with at the start: "Site walk at Example Foods, Smithfield, with Jo Bloggs." That is what finds the account in the CRM.
  3. Say each conveyor's name as you reach it, in a set phrase: "Now at CV-200, the spiral exit." The tools look for "now at", "now on", "we're at", "moving to", "next is" or "this is", followed by a conveyor name (CV-200, Line 3, Conveyor 12, PK-4).
  4. Say when you move to a project: "Now talking about the new packing line project." Photos taken then are kept apart from the last conveyor.
  5. Say what you are photographing, just before or after the photo: "Photo of the drive sprocket." This gives the caption and makes the match certain (high confidence means speech within 10 seconds of the photo).
  6. Log belts in Field CRM, or read them out in full: series, style, material, colour, rod, width, length, quantity, sprocket and bore. Anything not said shows as a gap on the quote request, and customer service will ask.
  7. Say decisions out loud with who and when: "Quote by Friday", "Jo's team will check the take-up at the next service." Follow-ups come only from what was said or written.
  8. Leave the phone's clock on automatic. If the phone's clock and the recorder's are out, every match is out (section 10).
  9. Photograph each page of handwritten notes flat, in good light, at the end of the walk. Write conveyor names as you said them.
  10. Ask before recording. Tell the people walking with you that you are recording for your notes, and follow the site's rules on photos.

5. After the call

  1. In Field CRM, log the call as usual: belts (the specification is easier there than said), notes, photos.
  2. On the PC, open the call in Field CRM. Click ⋯ beside Create and share, then Save for Claude. The first time, pick your PC folder.
  3. In Claude Desktop, type Do the call report, with the customer if you did more than one call that day.
  4. Read Claude's review list and check those points in the documents.
  5. Send the customer copy to the customer, and the site walk notes to customer service with the quote request.
  6. In Field CRM, open the call, click ⋯, then Import call report. Read the message saying what it adds, and click OK.
  7. Log the call in Dynamics with the site walk notes attached.

Phone photos reach the PC through OneDrive camera upload, which keeps their time. Photos sent through WhatsApp, Teams chat or email lose it.

Without folder access. If Claude cannot open your PC folders:

  1. In Field CRM, Save for Claude (it saves into the visit's folder, or on a phone downloads one file).
  2. Copy the visit's phone photos into the same folder.
  3. Right-click the folder, Send to, Compressed (zipped) folder.
  4. Drag the zip into the Claude chat and type Do the call report.
  5. Save the files Claude gives you back into the visit's folder, then carry on from step 4 above.

6. What Claude does

SKILL.md holds Claude's instructions; in short:

  1. Checks the tools (check_setup.py).
  2. Finds the visit's folder and reads Field CRM call.json (fieldcrm_call.py). With no Field CRM call, finds the customer in the CRM export (find_customer.py) and asks if it is not clear.
  3. Fetches the Plaud transcript and its start time.
  4. Copies the visit's photos from the camera roll by time (collect_photos.py), and sets aside photos of note pages and data sheets to read.
  5. Lines up the transcript and the photos (walk_timeline.py).
  6. Reads the handwritten notes, data sheets and drawings.
  7. Writes source\report.json.
  8. Draws the documents (build_report.py) and writes the file for Field CRM (make_import.py).
  9. Tells you where everything is, gives you the review list, and the next steps: send, import, Dynamics.

How it sorts the notes: conversations never go the same way twice, so Claude groups what belongs together by what it is about, not strictly by when it was said, and infers where a remark belongs from its context. Where it placed something that matters by inference, it says so on the review list.

Rules it follows: where things go is a judgement, the facts are not; facts only from the recording, notes, photos, Field CRM and the CRM export; never a guessed measurement, part, sprocket, date or name; belts keep Field CRM's values; anything internal only in the internal notes; Australian English; Intralox product names as Intralox writes them. It never changes Field CRM's files and never moves or deletes anything in your folders.

7. The tools

All are in scripts/, plain Python 3.8 or later. Each prints its options with --help.

check_setup.py

python3 scripts/check_setup.py

Says what this computer has: Python, the logo, Pillow (for reading photo times and shrinking photos), pillow-heif (for iPhone HEIC photos), openpyxl (for an .xlsx CRM export) and which PDF engines are available.

self_test.py

python3 scripts/self_test.py

Runs the tools on the test visit and checks the results against the known answers: the Field CRM call read with its belt's ref, the right photos collected by time, the right customer found, three conveyors and the project, each photo's conveyor and confidence, both documents made with nothing internal in the customer copy, and the file for Field CRM. Output goes to self-test-output/ in the skill's folder. Exit code 0 means passed.

fieldcrm_call.py

python3 scripts/fieldcrm_call.py "<visit folder>/Field CRM call.json" [--photos-out FOLDER] [--json]

Reads the call Field CRM's Save for Claude wrote (kind field-crm-call-for-claude, version 1) and prints it: the call's id, the customer (the CRM account) and whether it is in the CRM, the date, the account manager, the people on the call, every belt with its ref (its place in the call), the notes, other entries and the photos. --json prints the same as JSON. A file saved on a phone holds its photos inside it; --photos-out writes them out as files.

The file itself holds: call (id, customer, site, type, date and iso, mgr, manualAccount, summary, contacts with in_crm, entries each with its ref and its photos by file name, loose) and account (the CRM account's name, mgr and all its contacts).

collect_photos.py

python3 scripts/collect_photos.py --from CAMERA_ROLL --start TIME (--end TIME | --minutes N) --to FOLDER [--margin 600] [--dry-run]

Copies (never moves) every photo whose own time (EXIF) falls between --margin seconds before the start and after the end, default 10 minutes either side, into the visit's photos folder. A photo already there is not copied again. Photos with no time of their own that might be from the visit are named, not copied. --dry-run says what it would copy.

make_import.py

python3 scripts/make_import.py "<visit folder>/source/report.json" --out "<visit folder>"

Writes fieldcrm-import.json (kind field-crm-claude-report, version 1) for Field CRM's Import call report:

Field From report.json In Field CRM
callId fieldcrm.call_id The call it goes into; a different call asks first
summary notes The call's summary, which heads Field CRM's own call notes
internal_notes internal_notes A note, Internal notes, replaced on a later import
contacts contacts Added to the call if not there; in_crm false flags it
belts belts With ref: that belt's empty fields filled, nothing typed changed. Without: a new belt, updated (not duplicated) on a later import
photos, other_photos photos Inside the file, longest side 1600 px; under their belt, or the call's loose photos; one already on the call is skipped
follow_ups follow_ups task true: a next step on the planner, on date if given
review review Kept with the call
documents drawn again without photos Kept with the call: Open Claude's notes, Open Claude's customer copy

find_customer.py

python3 scripts/find_customer.py EXPORT --name TEXT --site TEXT --people NAME [NAME ...] [--top 5]
python3 scripts/find_customer.py EXPORT --account "Exact Account Name"
Option Meaning
EXPORT The CRM contacts export: .xlsx, .xlsm, or .csv (also read from a .txt holding CSV)
--name The customer as heard or written
--site The site or suburb as heard or written
--people People named on the walk
--top How many matches to show (default 5)
--account Print this one account as JSON: account, customer, site, account manager, contacts with role, email and mobile

The export it reads is the one Field CRM imports: one row per contact, with columns Account Name, Full Name (or First Name and Last Name), Job Role or Job Title, Email 1, Mobile and Account Manager. Column names are matched loosely, as Field CRM matches them. The site is the part of the account name after " - " ("Example Foods - Smithfield"). In a workbook, the first visible sheet with an "Account Name" column is used.

Matching forgives mishearing and word order. Scores: the customer name counts 3, the site 2, and each person heard who matches a contact at the account (0.8 or better) counts 1.5. The scores are for Claude to weigh; when two accounts are close, Claude asks you.

walk_timeline.py

python3 scripts/walk_timeline.py --transcript FILE --start TIME --photos FOLDER
         [--out timeline.json] [--window 30] [--photo-shift SECONDS]
Option Meaning
--transcript The transcript, as text or JSON (formats below)
--start When the recording started, local time: 2026-10-29 09:00:00, 2026-10-29T09:00, 29/10/2026 09:00. A time with a zone (2026-10-28T22:00:00Z, +00:00) is turned into the computer's local time
--photos The folder of site photos. Subfolders are included. JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF
--out Where to write the result. Default: timeline.json next to the photos folder
--window Seconds either side of a photo to collect speech from for its caption, and the limit for medium confidence. Default 30
--photo-shift Seconds added to every photo's time, to correct a phone clock that was out (section 10)

Transcript formats read:

  • Text, one remark per line starting with its time: 00:01:23 Speaker 1: words, [00:01:23] words, 01:23 words, or a time and speaker on one line with the words on the lines below (the Plaud app's text export).
  • JSON: a list of segments, or an object holding the list under segments, transcript, items, data, utterances or sentences. Each segment has its words in text, content, sentence or words, and its start in start, start_time, startTime, begin, offset or t (seconds, or hh:mm:ss), or start_ms, startMs, start_time_ms, begin_ms (milliseconds). Very large plain numbers are taken as milliseconds.

Where the walk is. A conveyor starts with "now at", "now on", "we're at", "moving to", "next is" or "this is" and a conveyor name. A project starts with "now talking about the … project" (also "discussing" or "onto"). Each photo is put with whichever was named last before it. That is a starting point: Claude moves a photo when what was said, the picture or the notes show it is of something else.

Photo times come from the photo's own EXIF date (DateTimeOriginal). If a photo has none, the file's modified date is used and the photo is noted "time from the file date, not the photo": treat its match as doubtful.

Confidence for each photo:

Rule
high Speech within 10 seconds of the photo
medium Speech within the window (30 seconds)
low No speech within the window
none Taken more than a minute before the recording or after it ended

timeline.json holds:

Field Meaning
recording_start Start time used, local
recording_length Time of the last remark, h:mm:ss
window_s The window used
conveyors[] Each conveyor named: conveyor, description (words after the name), at (into the recording), clock (time of day), said (the remark)
projects[] Each project named: project, at, clock, said
photos[] Each photo: file, taken, into_recording, conveyor or project (last named before it), said_near (remarks within the window, each with its time), nearest_speech_s, confidence, note
transcript[] Every remark: at, clock, speaker, text

build_report.py

python3 scripts/build_report.py report.json [--out FOLDER] [--only internal|customer]
         [--html-only] [--pdf-engine auto|chromium|weasyprint|reportlab]
Option Meaning
report.json The content (section 8). Photo paths in it are relative to this file's folder
--out Folder for the files. Default: the folder report.json is in
--only Make one of the two documents only
--html-only Make only the HTML
--pdf-engine Which PDF engine to use. Default auto: the first that works, in the order below

Files are named <Customer> - Site walk notes - <date> (internal) and <Customer> - Site walk report - <date> (customer copy), each .html and .pdf. It warns when a photo listed in photos is not used anywhere, or a photo used cannot be found or opened. The documents are still made, without it.

PDF engines, in the order tried:

  1. Chromium: Playwright for Python if installed, otherwise Edge, Chrome or Chromium if found. Prints the HTML itself, so the PDF matches the HTML exactly.
  2. WeasyPrint, if installed. Also prints the HTML.
  3. ReportLab: draws the same document natively, close to the HTML but not identical (fonts are Helvetica). In Claude's workspace this is usually the one used.

If none works, the HTML is still made: open it in Edge or Chrome, press Ctrl+P, choose Save as PDF, paper A4, and tick Background graphics.

9. The documents' look

The documents use Field CRM's own stylesheet (its compiled call notes and belt request for quote), so they look like the documents you already send:

  • A4 page, white, with the Intralox logo on the red band at the top. The logo is never recoloured, stretched or cropped.
  • Type: Roboto, then Arial, then Helvetica Neue, as Field CRM.
  • Section headings in Intralox cyan, uppercase, letter-spaced.
  • Ruled tables, label column in grey, cyan header bands on the belt table.
  • Each belt to quote in a block with a cyan stripe down its left edge.
  • Flags ("Needs adding to Dynamics", "not yet confirmed with TSG") in Intralox dark red text, on the internal copy only.
  • Photos two to a row, each with its time and caption.
  • A dark grey footer: document, customer, date.

The sections always come in this order: details, people, notes, internal notes (internal copy), photos, belts to quote.

Changing the look. The HTML's style is the CSS text in build_report.py, copied from Field CRM's NOTES_CSS; the ReportLab PDF has its own colours and sizes in pdf_reportlab(). Change both together, then run self_test.py and compare the HTML and the PDF. The logo is assets/intralox-logo.png; replace it only with an official Intralox file.

10. Troubleshooting

Claude cannot find the call. Check you clicked Save for Claude on the call, and that the PC folder Claude uses is the one Field CRM saves to (the settings file, section 3). The visit's folder is Customer\Site\date.

Collect photos found nothing, or the wrong ones. Check the camera roll path in the settings file, that OneDrive camera upload has finished on the phone, and that the recording's start time is right (local time).

Import call report says "Nothing new". The call already has everything in the report: it was imported before. A changed report imports the changes.

Import call report says it was made for another call. The report's call id is not this call's. Check you opened the right call; if it is right, click OK to import it anyway.

Claude picked the wrong customer, or asks which one. Say the customer and site clearly at the start of the walk (section 4), and check the export is a recent one. Tell Claude the right account; it draws the documents again.

Claude cannot find the CRM export (only needed for a call not in Field CRM). Check the Microsoft 365 connector is connected, and tell Claude the file's name or folder. Or attach the export to the chat.

"Reading .xlsx needs openpyxl". Ask Claude to run pip install openpyxl, or save the export as CSV.

Photos land under the wrong conveyor, all shifted the same way. The phone's clock and the recorder's were out. Find one photo whose true moment you know from the recording ("Photo of the drive sprocket" at 0:04:41, so 09:04:41), compare with the photo's time, and ask Claude to run walk_timeline.py again with --photo-shift set to the difference in seconds (positive if the photo's time is early).

Every photo says "time from the file date". The photos lost their EXIF data, usually by going through WhatsApp, Teams chat or email. Copy them off the phone again by OneDrive camera upload or a cable. If that is not possible, Claude places them by what they show and by the notes, and marks them all for checking.

"No conveyors found". The conveyor names were not said in one of the set phrases (section 4), or the transcript spelled them differently ("see V 200"). Claude can still sort the photos by what was said, with lower confidence. Tell Claude the conveyor names and roughly when you reached each.

A photo "could not be opened" and HEIC is mentioned. The photos are iPhone HEIC files. Ask Claude to run pip install pillow-heif and build again. To avoid it: on the iPhone, Settings, Camera, Formats, Most Compatible.

"PDF: not made". No PDF engine is available. Ask Claude to run pip install reportlab and build again; or use the HTML's Save as PDF (section 7). If Claude says it cannot install anything, your administrator has limited its network access for code execution; ask them to allow the Python package index (pypi.org), or use Save as PDF.

Claude cannot find the recording. Check the Plaud connector is connected (section 3), and that the recording has finished transcribing in the Plaud app. Or export the transcript and attach it.

The pasted document looks plain in Outlook. Outlook drops some styling when pasting. Attach the HTML or the PDF instead.

The handwriting was misread. Claude lists words it could not read; it can still misread one confidently. Check measurements and part numbers against your notes before sending.

11. Customer data and privacy

  • The call, the recording, the photos, the notes and the documents are customer information. They stay in your PC folders (synced to your work OneDrive), Field CRM (encrypted on the device before it syncs) and the Claude conversation (under Intralox's Claude agreement). The recording is also in Plaud's cloud under Plaud's terms.
  • Claude never reaches Field CRM's database: files in the visit folder are the only link, in both directions.
  • Field CRM calls are shared with your team, so the imported report, internal notes included, is seen by your team like the rest of the call.
  • This package contains no customer data: the test visit, its Field CRM call, its CRM export, the example and all names in them are made up. Do not add real visits, a real export or the belt catalogue to it.
  • Ask before recording, and follow each site's rules on photographs.

12. What has and has not been tested

Tested (on Linux, Python 3.11, and Field CRM's own test suite):

  • self_test.py passes: it reads the made-up Field CRM call and its belt's ref; it collects the right photos by time; it finds the customer in the made-up export ahead of the near misses; it matches the photos; it draws both documents (the customer copy without anything internal); and it writes fieldcrm-import.json for the right call, with the belt's ref, five photos, two next steps and documents without photos.
  • Field CRM's test suite (tests/qa-claude.mjs) runs this skill's make_import.py on the example and imports the result into a call: the logged belt is filled in from the walk without changing what was typed, the photos, contact and next steps come in, and importing again adds nothing.
  • Save for Claude writes the call and its photos into the visit's folder (a stand-in folder in the test, not real disk), or a download on a phone.

Not yet tested:

  • Running inside Claude Enterprise, and Claude Desktop's folder access.
  • The Plaud connector and a real Plaud transcript.
  • A real camera roll, real phone photos (HEIC, OneDrive's naming), real handwriting, a real visit.
  • The browser's real folder picker and Windows paths.

Check the first real reports carefully, and feed back what did not line up.
