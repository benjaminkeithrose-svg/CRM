Missing, worn or displaced wear strips CW-01

Observations
  • Wear strips missing over part of the carryway
  • Wear strips loose, broken or displaced
Risk of no action
  • Foreign material contamination
  • Reduced belt life
  • Increased downtime
Recommendation

Review and replace all affected wear strips and rollers. Blackening on food contact sides is a product contamination risk; on the underside it wears the belt prematurely and risks breakage.

Once corrected

Reduced foreign material contamination risk and extended belt life.

PA4192 (C113) — Carton incline
HighUrgent

Excessive catenary sag SG-01

Observations
  • First sag section deeper than specification
  • Belt jumping teeth at the drive
Risk of no action
  • Poor sprocket engagement
  • Reduced belt life
  • Increased belt bounce
Recommendation

Reduce sag depth to restore back tension. Additional catenary sag decreases belt tension and can lead to sprocket disengagement. Back tension increases as the sag section length increases and as sag depth decreases.

  • Sag depth — 1 in to 4 in (25 mm to 102 mm) for each catenary sag section, including the first section after the snub support

Once corrected

Reliable sprocket engagement and extended belt life.

  • Edge shaving or scalloping
Risk of no action
  • Foreign material contamination
  • Imminent belt failure

Recommendation
