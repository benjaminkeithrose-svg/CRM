1. Overview

Field CRM is the app Intralox's ANZ field sales team uses on the road and at the desk: planning visits, logging site calls with belts and health checks, quote requests, call notes for customers, tasks and emails, account plans, opportunities with their Blue Sheets, large account plans, and the CIP and MBOs.

It is one app that runs in a web browser: on an Android phone (Chrome), an iPhone (Safari, iOS 16.4 or later) and a PC (Chrome or Edge). It is installed from the browser like an app (a "progressive web app") and works with no signal: everything it needs, and all your data, is kept on the device. When there is signal it keeps the phone and the PC in step through a cloud database.

What happens where

  • Typing, logging, planning: on the device, instantly, with or without signal.
  • Keeping devices in step: through Supabase, automatically, when there is signal (see Sync and security).
  • AI (Tidy, Draft it, the coach, the visit summary, reading a spec sheet photo, setting up an opportunity from a description): through the ai-tidy function to Claude, only when you tap for it (see AI).
  • New versions of the app: from GitHub Pages when a pull request is merged; each device takes them after the app is closed and opened twice (see Running it).

The team

The app can be used by one person or by a team of 4 to 10. A team shares call reports, visits, plans, opportunities, products, saved notes and added contacts; the admin keeps the shared CRM export and reference data; tasks, planning weeks and the CIP/MBO stay private to each person. See the sharing table in Sync and security.

2. Database architecture

There are two databases. Each device (the phone, the PC) keeps a full copy of your data in the browser's own database. The cloud (Supabase) holds an encrypted copy of each record so the devices can find each other's changes. The device copy is the one the app works from; the cloud copy is only ever a go-between.

2.1 On the device

The browser database is IndexedDB, named fieldcrm, currently at version 9. It has 11 stores (think of each as a table). Each store holds records looked up by a key. The version number goes up whenever a store is added; the app moves an older database forward by itself (see Running it).

tasks, cip and weeks have no team rule, so they stay private. A person taken out of the team (removed_at) loses the team's data and key at once; the team keeps reading what they wrote.

Two server-side rules guard every write (the trigger on records):

  • Newest edit wins. A write carrying an older client_updated than the copy already there is skipped, so a device coming back online with stale data can never overwrite newer work.
  • A row keeps its owner and takes its owner's team.

The schema is in supabase/migrations/ (0001 to 0008), each file explaining its step. They hold no data and no secrets.

3. Sync and security

How the phone and the PC stay in step

sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Phone
  participant S as Supabase
  participant C as PC
  P->>P: You change a record. It is saved on the phone straight away and marked "waiting to send"
  Note over P: about 10 seconds later, with signal
  P->>P: Encrypt the record
  P->>S: Send it
  S->>S: Keep it only if it is newer than the copy already there
  S-->>C: "Something changed" (live, within a second or two, if the PC is open)
  C->>S: Ask for everything newer than it last read
  S->>C: The encrypted record
  C->>C: Decrypt it and save it on the PC

When a sync runs (nothing to press):

  • when the app opens;
  • when signal comes back;
  • about 10 seconds after a change (a burst of changes goes as one);
  • when you come back to the app after more than 20 seconds away;
  • within a second or two of another device changing something, while the app is open (Supabase Realtime tells it a row changed; the message carries no data, the device then fetches as normal).

What is sent. Records go one at a time (calls, visits, tasks, saved notes, products, plans, opportunities, CIP/MBO, added contacts). The CRM export, the belt reference, the audit register, zone overrides, reassignments and planning weeks go as whole datasets. Photos go to the photo store named by a hash of their contents, so the same photo is only ever sent once.

What is not sent. Settings that belong to one device: sign-in details, the manuals' page images, the import log.

When two devices change the same thing

The newer edit wins, record by record. Each record carries the time of its last real edit (updated, or touchedAt on a visit), and the server refuses to replace a copy with an older one. So:

  • Change a call on the phone, then a different call on the PC: both survive.
  • Change the same call on both: the later change wins, the earlier is lost.
  • Delete on one device: it is deleted on the other at its next sync. In a team, only the author or the admin can delete a shared record; anyone else's delete is ignored and the record comes back.

Encryption

Everything that leaves a device is encrypted on that device first. Supabase stores only scrambled text and cannot read your customers, notes or photos.

  • The data key. Each person (or the team) has one random key (AES-GCM, 256-bit). It encrypts every record and photo. Each record is also compressed first, and tied to its owner, store and ID, so a scrambled row cannot be moved onto another record.
  • The passphrase. The data key is kept in your vault in Supabase, locked with a key made from your passphrase (PBKDF2 with SHA-256, 600,000 rounds). The passphrase never leaves the device. Each device asks for it once, then keeps the unlocked key on that device.
  • A team shares one data key, locked with the team passphrase in the admin's vault. Members can read that vault; nobody outside the team can.
  • On the device the data is not encrypted by the app: it relies on the phone's or PC's own lock and disk encryption. A lost phone that is locked keeps it safe; an unlocked one does not.

Sign-in is by email and password (Supabase Auth). Without a sign-in, nothing in the cloud can be read or written at all.

4. AI

Every AI feature goes through one server function, ai-tidy, in Supabase. The app itself never holds the Anthropic key: a key in a public web page would be anyone's. The function checks you are signed in, counts the request, sends it to Claude, and returns the answer. It stores nothing: not what was sent, not the answer. The app always shows the answer for checking before it changes anything.

  • Model: Claude Haiku 5.5 (claude-haiku-5-5), Ben's choice.
  • Limit: 200 requests a day per person, counted in ai_usage; it resets at midnight UTC. The count holds no text.
  • Needs: signal and a cloud sync sign-in.
  • Cost: a few cents a month at normal use (priced in IDEAS.md).

How the coach thinks

The coach and the brief are told to draw on Ben's books: New Sales. Simplified. (Weinberg), Value First, Then Price (Hinterhuber and Snelgrove), The New Successful Large Account Management (Miller and Heiman), Strategic Selling for the Blue Sheet, Influence (Cialdini), The Challenger Customer (Adamson and Dixon), SPIN Selling (because the CIP sets it) and The Speed of Trust. They tie advice to Intralox's mission and three commitments (customer efficiencies, downtime, continuous improvement), and must never invent people, numbers, prices, dates or promises.

The wording is in supabase/functions/ai-tidy/index.ts; changing it means deploying the function again (see Running it).

Export for Claude

Separate from the features above, Export for Claude (Reports) writes text files of your accounts, calls, opportunities and plans for a Claude Project. It can replace people's names with their roles. The files go only where you put them.

Backup and restore

Back up (Settings, Backup and restore) writes one file with every store, the settings that matter, and optionally the photos. Contacts added in the app go in it separately from the accounts.

Restore adds and updates records by their IDs; it never deletes anything already on the device. With cloud sync on, a restored record then syncs like any other change.

Cloud sync is itself a second copy of everything except device-only settings, so a new or wiped device gets everything back by signing in and entering the passphrase. It is not a backup, though: a delete reaches every device. The backup file is the undo.

6. Running it

Releasing a change

The app's code lives on GitHub (repository benjaminkeithrose-svg/CRM). The main branch is what GitHub Pages publishes, so anything merged into main is live within a minute or two.

  1. The change is made on a working branch, never on main.
  2. The version goes up by one, in three places that must agree: <meta name="build"> in index.html, APP_BUILD in app.js, and CACHE in sw.js (fieldcrm-vNN).
  3. The Help screen is updated if a flow or button changed, and MANUAL.md is made again from it (python3 tools/help-to-manual.py).
  4. These documents are updated if the data, sync or AI changed, and the printable copy is made again (python3 tools/docs-to-print.py).
  5. The tests run (npm test) and all pass.
  6. A pull request is opened that says, in plain language, what changed, which files, what was tested and what to check on the phone.
  7. Ben reads it and says "Merge it". Nothing is merged without that.
  8. After the merge, each device takes the new version (below).

Why the version matters. The service worker keeps a copy of the app on each device and serves that copy, so the app opens with no signal. It only fetches new files when the version in sw.js changes. A change merged without a version bump never reaches the phone.

Getting the new version on a device

The app checks once a day, when it opens with signal, and updates itself if the server has a newer version. To get it straight away:

  1. Tap ⋯ on Home, then Settings.
  2. Tap Get the latest version.

Or close the app fully and open it again, twice: the first opening fetches the new files, the second runs them. Nothing on the device is touched by an update: calls, accounts, photos and settings stay.

When the database version goes up

Some changes add a new kind of record, and the device database then needs a new version (it is at 9). The new build opens the database at the higher version and adds the new store; nothing already in it is changed.

An older copy of the app still open in another tab, or left running in the background, holds the database at the old version. Since v126:

  • the new build waits and shows "Close any other Field CRM window or tab to finish the update", and carries on the moment the old copy closes;
  • the old copy, if it is open, closes the database and reloads itself into the new build.

Before v126 the new build started with no accounts and no belt data in that case (v125, 2026-10-12). Nothing was lost; closing the other tab brought it back. tests/qa-upgrade.mjs guards this.

Tests

npm install     # once per cloud session: jsdom and fake-indexeddb

Each suite loads the real index.html and scripts into a simulated browser (jsdom) with a simulated database, drives the screens, and checks the result. They catch runtime errors, broken flows and escaping faults. They cannot test the camera, the share sheet, the microphone, touch, launcher icons, the clipboard under corporate policy, or a real 5,700-row export.

Known issues

  • The Plant Audit Template FORM v1.2 will not import (it has no COLOR_IND column, a separate dimension sheet, and a different sprocket layout). Plant_Audit_Template_1.xlsm still imports.
  • COLOR_IND in the audit data is colours found in plants, not colours a belt can be ordered in; it does not restrict the colour picker. The same holds for materials.
  • The Android back gesture moves back through history; the on-screen back button goes up the app's structure. Deliberate.
  • pdf.js, used only when importing an engineering manual, loads from a CDN on first use.
  • On an iPhone (v135 onwards): a file can't be shared into the app from another app (iOS has no share target for web apps), so imports use the app's own buttons; the microphone button depends on Safari's speech support and may not work in the home-screen app, where the keyboard microphone always does; text boxes keep their fixed size with their own scroll. The app must be added to the Home Screen: a Safari tab can lose its data after a week unused. Not yet tried on a real iPhone.

Rules that bite

  • Write only through the store functions (callsPut, oppsPut, kvSet and so on). They mark the change for cloud sync; a direct write never reaches the other devices.
  • Bump updated (touchedAt on a visit) on a real edit, or an older copy on another device can win.
  • Never assume the database is open. Every operation awaits ready().
  • Quote requests are rectype:'quote', never kind. Read calls through callsAll() and quotes through quotesAll().
  • Applying a record from the cloud sets cloudQuiet, or it is marked as a change and sent straight back.
  • No customer data, belt catalogue, CIP/MBO wording or keys in the repo, tests included. Tests use made-up names.

Checklist: adding a new kind of record (a store)

  1. app.js, openDB: raise DB_VER by one and add the store in onupgradeneeded with a comment naming the app version.
  2. app.js: add xAll, xPut, xDel beside the others (storeAll/storePut/storeDel).
  3. Cloud sync, all in the cloud sync part: add the store to CLOUD_REC_STORES; to the store list in cloudSeed; to both lookup tables in cloudApplyRec (delete and put); and to each stats and up counter object (search for people: 0).
  4. Backup and restore: add it to buildBackup and to doRestore.
  5. If it is shared with the team: a new numbered migration in supabase/migrations/ with read, add and change policies on records for that store (copy 0008_team_people.sql). If it is private, nothing: private is the default. Never add cip or tasks to a team rule.
  6. CLAUDE.md: the IndexedDB version and store list in the Files table, and the store functions in the cloud sync rule. These documents: 02-database.md (the store table and its field list) and, if shared, the sharing table in 03-sync-and-security.md.
  7. Tests: tests/qa-upgrade.mjs opens an old database version and checks the upgrade; add the new store to what it checks. Add a suite for the feature.

Checklist: adding a dialog

  1. Add the <dialog id="..."> to index.html.
  2. Add its id to DIALOGS in app.js, so history navigation can close it.
  3. Open it with the guard, then name the history entry: if(d.showModal) d.showModal(); else d.setAttribute('open',''); pushDialog(id); Never a bare history.pushState.

Checklist: adding a screen

  1. Add <section class="scr" id="s-NAME"> to index.html.
  2. Add NAME to TITLES (header title) and PARENT (where back goes) in app.js.
  3. Open it with go('NAME').
  4. Add the Help section (s-help), then run python3 tools/help-to-manual.py.

The microphone on a text box

Mark the box data-talk="FIELD", where FIELD is the kind of box Tidy should expect (note, sales, line, self and the others listed in supabase/functions/ai-tidy/index.ts, FIELD). If the box is inside one of the watched containers (search micAuto), the microphone appears by itself, also for boxes drawn later. Read-only boxes are skipped.

Changing what the AI does

The instructions to Claude are in supabase/functions/ai-tidy/index.ts: the shared METHOD (the books, the mission and commitments), one entry per feature in KINDS, and one per kind of text box in FIELD. A change there needs the function deployed again (see Running it). tests/qa-ai.mjs and the coach suites check the app's side with a stand-in for the function.

Writing a test

Copy the top of an existing suite (tests/qa-people.mjs is a short one). It loads index.html into jsdom with runScripts:'dangerously', adds the scripts as real <script> elements, and uses fake-indexeddb. Top-level let and const are not on window: read them with w.eval('(NAME)'). Print PASS n and FAIL n and exit non-zero on a failure; tests/run.mjs picks the suite up by its qa- name.

Some element IDs are referred to but absent, harmlessly and guarded: pastList, looseCam, looseGal, indentAllLnk, detachBtn.

8. Your data

A short page for anyone using Field CRM.

Where it is kept

  • On your phone and your PC. Everything you enter is saved on the device the moment you enter it, and works with no signal.
  • In the cloud, encrypted, if you sign in to cloud sync. That is what keeps your phone and PC in step. It is scrambled on your device before it leaves, with a key only your passphrase unlocks, so the cloud provider (Supabase) cannot read it.
  • On an iPhone, only once it is on the Home Screen. A Safari tab can lose its data after a week unused; the home-screen app keeps it.
  • Nowhere else. The app's code is public on GitHub; none of your data is ever in it.

Who can see it

WhatWho sees it
Your call reports, visits, account plans, opportunities, saved notes, products, contacts you addedYou, and your team if you are in one
Your tasks and emails, planning weeksYou only
Your CIP and MBOsYou only
The CRM export, belt reference, audit registerYour team; the admin keeps them up to date

In a team, colleagues can read and change the shared records; only the person who wrote a record, or the admin, can delete it.

AI

AI only runs when you tap for it (Tidy, Draft it, Write summary, Prepare, Coach me, reading a spec sheet photo, setting up an opportunity from a description). What you tapped on is sent to Claude, the answer comes back, and nothing is kept: not by the app's server, not by Claude. You always see the answer before anything changes. Each person has 200 a day.

The microphone uses your phone's own speech recognition (Google's, on Android), which needs signal.

Keeping it safe

  1. Keep your phone locked. The data on it is protected by the phone's own lock.
  2. Keep your cloud sync passphrase in a password manager. It cannot be recovered; without it the cloud copy cannot be read.
  3. Make a backup now and then: tap ⋯ on Home, then Settings, then Backup and restore, then Back up. Keep the file somewhere other than the phone. Cloud sync is not a backup: a delete reaches every device.
  4. Never put an export, a backup or a Dynamics file anywhere public.

Getting it back

On a new or wiped phone, install the app, sign in to cloud sync and enter your passphrase. Your records come back. To undo a mistake, restore from a backup file: it adds back what is missing and never deletes anything.

Leaving the team: the admin removes your sign-in. What you wrote stays with the team.
