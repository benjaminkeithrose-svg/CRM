# 4. AI

Every AI feature goes through one server function, `ai-tidy`, in Supabase.
The app itself never holds the Anthropic key: a key in a public web page would
be anyone's. The function checks you are signed in, counts the request, sends
it to Claude, and returns the answer. It stores nothing: not what was sent,
not the answer. The app always shows the answer for checking before it changes
anything.

- **Model:** Claude Haiku 5.5 (`claude-haiku-5-5`), Ben's choice.
- **Limit:** 200 requests a day per person, counted in `ai_usage`; it resets
  at midnight UTC. The count holds no text.
- **Needs:** signal and a cloud sync sign-in.
- **Cost:** a few cents a month at normal use (priced in `IDEAS.md`).

**Writing out a recording** (v166, a trial, Settings, Talking into the app) is not Claude and sends nothing: Whisper runs on the phone itself, and only its text goes on to Tidy or Sort it as usual. Claude's API does not take audio.

## What each feature sends

| Feature | Where | Sent to Claude | Comes back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tidy** | Beside a text box; or automatically after the microphone | That one box's text, and which kind of box it is | The tidied text, following spoken instructions such as "make that a list" |
| **Draft it** | An email task | Your spoken points, and the task's title, contact, account and subject | A subject and an email body |
| **Write summary** | A call | The call: account, date, people seen, what was logged | The summary that heads the call notes |
| **Read a spec sheet photo** | Belt form | The photo | The belt's fields, and anything it could not read |
| **Prepare** (brief) | A visit | The account, its records and plan, the visit's details | A pre-visit brief with SPIN questions |
| **Coach me** | A call | The call and the account's earlier records | Coaching and suggested account plan changes |
| **Coach** | An opportunity's Blue Sheet | The Blue Sheet, its map, and the account's records | Coaching and suggested Blue Sheet additions |
| **Set up from a description** | New opportunity, or the map | Your description, the customer's name and today's date (never the customer list) | Name, objective, value, close, parties and people, to tick |
| **Capture** (v165) | The Capture tile on Home (a bar at the top until v169): Sort it, or Finish on the microphone | The words said or typed, today's date and weekday, and the task type names (never the customer list) | Each piece as a task, phone call, site visit or note, with the company and person as said, date, time, length, details and the words it came from. The app matches the customer and contact itself, and shows each as a card to check before anything is added |

**The account's records**, for all three coaching features, include the
large account plan when the site is one of its sites (v148): the plan's
vision, goals, insights, objectives, strategies, open actions, SWOT and
critical success factors; where the site sits (corporate, division, group
engineering and functions, OEMs, the account team leader); and up to 30
people over the site, with their part, stance and who of ours covers them.
People hidden as not relevant are left out. Since v150 the coach is also told which large account objective a Blue Sheet delivers and a site plan supports, and asked to check that they work towards it. Nothing new leaves the app that
was not already in the plan.

**The microphone** is not AI on the way in: the phone's own speech
recognition writes the words into the box (on Android that is Google's, and it
needs signal). When you tap Finish, Tidy runs on what was written.

## How the coach thinks

The coach and the brief are told to draw on Ben's books: *New Sales.
Simplified.* (Weinberg), *Value First, Then Price* (Hinterhuber and
Snelgrove), *The New Successful Large Account Management* (Miller and Heiman),
*Strategic Selling* for the Blue Sheet, *Influence* (Cialdini), *The
Challenger Customer* (Adamson and Dixon), *SPIN Selling* (because the CIP sets
it) and *The Speed of Trust*. They tie advice to Intralox's mission and three
commitments (customer efficiencies, downtime, continuous improvement), and
must never invent people, numbers, prices, dates or promises.

The wording is in `supabase/functions/ai-tidy/index.ts`; changing it means
deploying the function again (see [Running it](06-running-it.md)).

## Export for Claude

Separate from the features above, **Export for Claude** (Reports) writes text
files of your accounts, calls, opportunities and plans for a Claude Project.
It can replace people's names with their roles. The files go only where you
put them.
